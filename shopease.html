🛒 ShopEase

Modern Flutter e-commerce mobile application designed to deliver a seamless shopping experience with elegant UI, wishlist support, cart management, and scalable architecture.

✨ Key Features

🛍 Product Browsing

Browse products with a clean, intuitive, and user-friendly shopping experience.

❤️ Wishlist

Save favorite products for later and personalize the shopping journey.

🛒 Shopping Cart

Add products, manage quantities, and review totals before checkout.

🌙 Dark Mode

Switch between beautifully designed light and dark themes.

⚙ Tech Stack

Flutter Dart State Management Local Storage Clean Architecture
