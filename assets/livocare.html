🏥 LivoCare

AI-Powered Healthcare Application for NAFLD Risk Prediction & Patient Management

LivoCare is a smart healthcare mobile application built for doctors to streamline patient assessment and predict Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD) risk.

The system allows physicians to either enter patient clinical data manually or upload medical images for AI-assisted liver risk analysis.

The application provides real-time predictions, patient analytics, risk categorization, and personalized medical recommendations to support faster and smarter clinical decision-making.

✨ Key Features

👨‍⚕ Patient Registration & Management

Add, update, and manage patient medical records efficiently.

📸 Image-Based Prediction

Upload medical images for automated AI-assisted liver risk assessment.

📊 Clinical Analytics Dashboard

Visualize patient statistics, risk distribution, and valuable health insights.

🧠 Risk Prediction Engine

Analyze patient health indicators to estimate NAFLD risk level in real time.

📋 Personalized Recommendations

Generate actionable clinical recommendations based on prediction outcomes.

🔐 Secure Authentication

Protected login system for healthcare professionals and doctors.
