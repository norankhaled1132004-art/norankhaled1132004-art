👨‍⚕ Patient Management

📸 AI Image Analysis

Upload diagnostic medical images for AI-assisted analysis.

📊 Analytics Dashboard

Track patient insights and health metrics visually.

🧠 Risk Prediction

Generate real-time NAFLD risk predictions.

📋 Smart Recommendations

Personalized healthcare recommendations for better decisions.

🔐 Secure Authentication

Protected access designed for healthcare professionals.
