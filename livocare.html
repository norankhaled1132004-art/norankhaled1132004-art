🏥 LivoCare

AI-Powered Healthcare Application for NAFLD Risk Prediction & Smart Patient Management

LivoCare is an intelligent healthcare mobile application designed for doctors to streamline patient management and support faster clinical decisions.

Physicians can manually enter patient medical data or upload diagnostic images for AI-assisted analysis to assess the risk of Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD).

The platform provides real-time risk prediction, analytics dashboards, patient tracking, and personalized healthcare recommendations.

✨ Key Features

👨‍⚕ Patient Management

Register, update, and manage patient records efficiently in one centralized workflow.

📸 AI Image Analysis

Upload medical images for intelligent AI-assisted liver disease risk assessment.

📊 Analytics Dashboard

Track patient insights, health metrics, and risk distribution visually.

🧠 Risk Prediction

Generate real-time NAFLD risk predictions using patient clinical indicators.

📋 Smart Recommendations

Provide actionable healthcare recommendations based on analysis results.

🔐 Secure Authentication

Protected access designed specifically for healthcare professionals.
